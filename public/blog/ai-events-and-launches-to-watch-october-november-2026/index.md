# AI Events and Launches to Watch, October to November 2026

URL: https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/ai-events-and-launches-to-watch-october-november-2026/
Published: 2026-10-05
Summary: A dated guide to the AI conferences, launch milestones and policy deadlines coming up in October and November 2026, and how to follow them without chasing hype.

**In short:** October and November 2026 bring a dense run of AI developer conferences, a phased frontier-model release still in progress and two US policy deadlines. Conferences are where tools and roadmaps get shown, but a talk is not a product launch, so treat announcements as claims until you can use the thing yourself.

## Key Takeaways

- Between October 12 and November 27 there are at least eight notable AI and developer events across North America and Europe, from New York to Vilnius.
- Gemini 4 Argon is still in phased release with no general-availability date, according to the reports on its announcement.
- Two US policy clocks are running: a 60-day drafting deadline and a 120-day report deadline, both counted from announcements we cannot pin to a single start date.
- A conference keynote is a claim. Wait for availability, pricing and independent testing before changing plans.

## Conferences in October

All dates and places below are as published by each organiser on the date of writing.

- **October 12-14, New York: AI Engineer New York.** An AI conference focused on production AI in financial services. *Why it matters:* a look at how regulated, risk-sensitive teams actually deploy AI.
- **October 16-25, Bay Area: Open Source AI Week.** Presented by the Agentic AI Foundation, the Linux Foundation and PyTorch, it bundles conferences, hackathons and startup showcases. *Why it matters:* it is the main gathering point for the open-source side of AI that week.
- **October 20-21, San Jose: PyTorch Conference North America.** Run by the PyTorch Foundation and part of Open Source AI Week. *Why it matters:* PyTorch is the framework behind much of today's open model work.
- **October 22-23, San Jose: AGNTCon + MCPCon North America.** Also part of Open Source AI Week, listed on the Linux Foundation calendar. *Why it matters:* it covers agent and tool-connection standards, the plumbing behind [AI agents getting their own infrastructure](/blog/ai-agents-are-getting-their-own-infrastructure/).
- **October 27-29: ODSC AI West.** The organiser lists the San Francisco Bay Area (Hyatt Regency SFO) as the venue, with 9 tracks and more than 250 speakers. *Why it matters:* a broad, practitioner-level view of what teams are building.

## Conferences in November

- **November 9-12, Lisbon: Web Summit.** A large general technology conference. *Why it matters:* it shows what investors and startups are prioritising, though it is less technical than the developer events above.
- **November 10-12, San Francisco: AI Engineer Code Summit.** An application-based conference focused on coding agents, development tools and production workflows. *Why it matters:* it is aimed squarely at how software teams use AI to write and ship code.
- **November 24-27, Vilnius: Big Data Conference Europe.** Workshops on November 24 in Vilnius only, then the main conference on November 25-27, available onsite and online. *Why it matters:* a European data-engineering and AI view, with an online option.

## Launches and Deadlines to Watch

These are not conference talks. Each one has a published source.

- **Gemini 4 Argon: general availability.** As reported by Ars Technica, Google announced the model on September 30 but is releasing it in phases, starting with a small group of trusted testers, with no specific timeline promised. General availability is said to begin with paid API users and Google AI Ultra subscribers. *Why it matters:* only general availability is safe to build on. See [how to read a frontier model release](/blog/gemini-4-argon-how-to-read-a-frontier-model-release/).
- **US Super Intelligence Force report.** TechCrunch reported on October 4 that the new task force has 120 days to report on risks and opportunities. If the clock starts on October 4, that lands around February 1, 2027. That is our arithmetic, since the reports do not give a start date. *Why it matters:* it may shape US policy language. Read our [explainer on the order](/blog/trump-super-intelligence-force-what-the-executive-order-says/).
- **Legislative definition of "Super Intelligence".** Fox News reported that the executive order, dated September 29, gives officials 60 days to draft legislative language defining the terms. Counted from September 29, that is around November 28, again our arithmetic. *Why it matters:* it is a naming and definitions change, not proof that superintelligence exists. Our [superintelligence explainer](/blog/what-is-superintelligence-and-why-is-it-called-that/) covers the concept.
- **EU AI Act dates.** These are covered in a separate guide: [what applies now and what was delayed](/blog/eu-ai-act-what-applies-now-and-what-was-delayed/).

## How to Follow Along Without Chasing Hype

1. **Use the organiser's page** for dates and venues, and re-check a week before you travel.
2. **Separate announced from available.** Ask whether you can use it today, at what price and under what terms.
3. **Wait for independent tests** before treating a benchmark as fact.
4. **Keep a short watchlist** of the three or four items that affect your work, and ignore the rest.
5. **Keep your systems model-flexible**, so a new release is a test you can run, not a rewrite.

## The Short Version

The next two months are busy, but only a few items change what a business can actually do: general availability of new models, and the policy deadlines that decide what you must follow. Treat conferences as a preview, check each source yourself, and move when something is available, priced and tested.

## FAQ

**Which AI conferences are happening in October and November 2026?**
As published by the organisers: AI Engineer New York (October 12-14), Open Source AI Week in the Bay Area (October 16-25) with PyTorch Conference North America (October 20-21), ODSC AI West (October 27-29), Web Summit in Lisbon (November 9-12), AI Engineer Code Summit in San Francisco (November 10-12) and Big Data Conference Europe in Vilnius (November 25-27).

**Is Gemini 4 Argon available to everyone yet?**
Not according to the reports on its announcement. Google is releasing it in phases, starting with a small group of trusted testers, and has not promised a specific timeline. General availability is said to begin with paid API users and Google AI Ultra subscribers.

**How do I tell a real AI launch from a conference announcement?**
Check whether you can use it today, at what price and under what terms. A keynote or demo is a claim. A generally available product with published pricing and independent testing is something you can build on.
