## 6. Pre-built industry applications ("AI for X" pattern)

Card format per industry: **"AI for [Industry]"** title + one outcome-first sentence + `Learn more` CTA (sometimes paired with a named report/guide CTA).

- **AI for Banking** — "Coordinate self-service and agent support with shared context, consistency, and built-in compliance."
- **AI for Healthcare** — "Enable 24/7 patient and member self-service and reduce staff burden with HIPAA-compliant intelligent assistance."
- **AI for Retail** — "Frictionless shopping and optimized operations with instant, personalized support at every touchpoint."
- **AI for IT** — "Resolve incidents, reduce ticket volume, and deliver 24/7 IT support"
- **AI for HR** — "Automate, resolve, and reduce employee queries and manual workload."
- **AI for Recruiting** — "Automate screening, accelerate hiring, and engage top talent."

> Formula: **[verb] + [what it does] + [compliance/trust qualifier if regulated industry]**. Every sentence names the mechanism (self-service, automation) AND the constraint that makes it enterprise-credible (compliance, HIPAA, 24/7). This is the direct model for our own Industry Showcase card copy.

## 7. Platform capability grid ("Leverage AI with AI") — 9 pillars

**Headline:** "Leverage AI with AI"
**Subhead:** "Nine ways the Kore.ai Agent Platform does the work."

Each pillar = **[Verb] with AI** name + a short contrarian/punchy tagline + one supporting sentence + a stat-flavored badge:

1. **Build with AI** — "Months to days. Not a roadmap. A runtime." / Badge: "Months to days"
2. **Orchestrate with AI** — "AI that handles the workflows other platforms can't." / Badge: "REAL-WORLD AI"
3. **Prove with AI** — "Compliance that doesn't need to trust the AI." / Badge: "Zero compliance flags"
4. **Test with AI** — "No more discovering broken AI in front of customers." / Badge: "Zero surprises"
5. **Deploy with AI** — "Scale the programme. Not the headcount." / Badge: "More AI shipped"
6. **Govern with AI** — "Complete audit trails, ready when you need them." / Badge: "100% audited"
7. **Scale with AI** — "A protected AI investment in a shifting industry." / Badge: "No lock-in"
8. **Simplify with AI** — "Two AI systems? You only need one." / Badge: "ONE VENDOR"
9. **Optimize with AI** — "Prove which agents delivered value." / Badge: ">5% EBIT GAIN"

> **This is the single most useful pattern for our Platform Pillars gap.** Each tagline is a short, punchy, slightly contrarian claim (names an old pain, implies it's solved) — not a feature description. The badge is a tiny standalone proof-point, not a repeated CTA. If we're writing the missing 4th pillar card ("Own it completely"), this formula — punchy tagline + one grounding sentence + a short proof badge — is the shape to match, filled with our own real claims.

## Overall copy voice observations

- Headlines lean **abstract/aspirational**, not feature-first ("Great experiences are built on a strong foundation" not "AI agent builder platform").
- Every value-prop sentence is **verb-first** and names a concrete mechanism, not a vague benefit.
- Regulated-industry copy always pairs the outcome with a **trust/compliance qualifier** in the same sentence (HIPAA, built-in compliance, SOC 2).
- Stats are used as **tiny standalone badges**, not narrated in prose — "100% audited," ">5% EBIT GAIN" sit next to the pillar, not woven into the paragraph.
- No FAQ pattern on marketing pages — don't force one to match Kore.ai; keep following ServiceNow's FAQ format for that gap instead.
