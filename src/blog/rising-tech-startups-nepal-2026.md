## Tech Startups in Nepal

Beyond established companies, Nepal has a growing startup ecosystem. These companies are building products for both local and global markets.

### Khalti

**Founded**: 2017 | **Location**: Lalitpur | **Team**: 150+

**Specialization**: Digital wallet, payments, fintech

Khalti is Nepal's second-largest digital wallet, known for developer-friendly APIs and merchant services.

**Notable work**:
- Digital wallet with millions of users
- Payment gateway for merchants
- Developer APIs for payment integration

**Why they made the list**: Strong growth and technical approach to fintech.

**Website**: khalti.com

### Hamro Patro

**Founded**: 2010 | **Location**: Kathmandu | **Team**: 80+

**Specialization**: Calendar app, content platform, news aggregation

Hamro Patro evolved from a simple calendar app into Nepal's most-used local content platform.

- Most downloaded Nepali app
- News and content aggregation
- Local language NLP features

**Why they made the list**: Massive local user base, evolving into a platform company.
