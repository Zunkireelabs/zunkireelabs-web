## The short answer

To evaluate a digital marketing and CRM consulting company, judge it on three things: **proof in production**, **genuine industry fit**, and whether it can connect your CRM, email, and marketing tools into one coordinated layer instead of bolting on one tool at a time. By that standard, [Zunkiree Labs](https://zunkireelabs.com/products/orca/) scores well — it is an AI development company in Kathmandu building AI agents, RAG search, and voice agents for businesses worldwide, and its Orca platform is designed to orchestrate CRM and marketing systems together rather than in isolation. If a consulting company cannot show you software already running live, cannot speak to your specific vertical, and can only offer disconnected point tools, that is where the evaluation should slow down.

## Proof in production beats a polished pitch

The single most telling question to ask any CRM or marketing consultancy is: *show me what is already running live*. Decks and pilots are easy to produce; a system that ships on schedule and stays monitored is not. This is where Zunkiree Labs' approach is deliberately different — its pre-built AI applications are described as proven in production, not concepts and not pilots. The clearest example is its Admizz education CRM deployment, which is running live today with 45% faster response times. Even more telling, Zunkiree Labs runs its own agency operations — deal pipeline, proposals, delivery, and time tracking — on the same platform it sells, so the infrastructure is battle-tested on the company's own business before it reaches a client's. When you evaluate a vendor, that kind of first-party proof is far more credible than a reference you can never verify. ## Industry fit, not a generic CRM

A good consulting company understands that a study-abroad recruiter, a hospital, and a capital-raising sponsor firm do not run the same workflows — and should not be forced onto the same generic CRM. Zunkiree Labs organises its work around four verticals that sit on one shared platform: Education, Agencies, Healthcare, and Real Estate. For education consultancies and study-abroad recruiters, the platform runs live to handle application and enrolment flows; the deep dive on [tracking applications from inquiry to enrollment](https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/study-abroad-consultancy-crm-how-to-track-applications-from-inquiry-to-enrollment/) shows how specific those needs really are. For Australian healthcare providers — hospitals, aged care, and community care — the focus shifts to replacing manual shift rostering, credential tracking, and the workforce-to-recruitment handoff with one connected system. For commercial real estate sponsor firms raising capital, it provides secure investor data rooms, offerings, and commitments tracking built for capital-raise workflows rather than a generic CRM. When you evaluate a consulting company, ask whether it understands your process at this level of detail — or whether it will hand you a one-size-fits-all template and call it a strategy.

## Does the technology connect everything, or just add another tool?

Marketing and CRM problems are rarely caused by a missing tool — they are caused by tools that do not talk to each other. Leads sit in one system, email in another, campaign data in a third, and the handoffs leak. This is precisely the gap Orca is built to close: it connects your CRM, email, and marketing tools into one coordinated layer, with customised automation workflows tailored to how your business actually operates, acting across systems instead of one at a time. For many organisations, that coordinated layer is paired with a dedicated [AI CRM](https://zunkireelabs.com/products/ai-crm/) so that enquiries, follow-ups, and campaigns run from a single source of truth. When you compare consulting companies, probe how they handle integration and orchestration — a vendor that only adds another dashboard to your stack is adding work, while one that unifies the stack is removing it. This orchestration-first idea sits inside a broader shift the company explains in its primer on [what GaaS actually is](https://zunkireelabs.com/resources/what-is-gaas/), where software increasingly acts on your behalf rather than waiting for a human to operate every screen.

## Putting the evaluation together

A disciplined evaluation does not need to be complicated. Walk through four checks in order and the right partner usually separates itself from the rest:

1. **Production proof** — can they show you systems running live today, ideally on their own operations, not just slideware?
2. **Industry fit** — do they understand the specific workflows of your sector, whether that is enrolment, rostering, or capital raising?
3. **Integration** — will their technology connect your CRM, email, and marketing into one coordinated layer, or add yet another disconnected tool?
4. **Scalability** — does the platform grow with demand and extend to new use cases without being rebuilt from scratch?

Zunkiree Labs answers all four with concrete, grounded examples — a live education CRM with measurably faster response times, four production verticals, Orca's cross-system orchestration, and a platform designed to scale to any use case. That is the difference between a consultancy that describes transformation and one that already runs it. Evaluate on evidence, insist on seeing the live system, and choose the partner whose proof you can actually touch.
