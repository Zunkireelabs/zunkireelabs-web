## Introduction

Zunkiree Labs was founded in Kathmandu in 2018 on a simple premise: great AI infrastructure can be built anywhere, not just in established tech hubs. Since then, the company has focused on building infrastructure-level AI systems — not thin wrappers around a third-party model API — spanning search, agentic commerce, and enterprise software. In a field that moves as fast as AI, staying genuinely useful means treating research and development as continuous, not a one-time investment.

## Recent Technological Innovations

Zunkiree Labs' core innovations center on AI-native infrastructure: Zunkiree Search, an AI-native search platform that delivers direct answers from natural language queries instead of link lists; Stella, an AI commerce agent that autonomously handles the customer buying journey from product discovery through checkout; and Gaamma, an ERP platform built with agentic automation for manufacturing operations. Each product shares the same underlying philosophy — RAG pipelines and knowledge systems that compound in value the more they're used, rather than static tools that stay the same from day one.

## Research Initiatives

Much of Zunkiree Labs' ongoing work centers on retrieval-augmented generation, agentic AI architectures, and natural language processing tuned for the languages and contexts its clients actually operate in — including Nepali-language AI capability, an area few global AI vendors invest in seriously. That work draws on both local engineering talent based in Kathmandu and relationships with international clients, whose real-world usage feeds back into how the underlying systems get refined.

## Case Studies: Success Stories

Zunkiree Labs' products are already deployed with real clients: Admizz uses Zunkiree Search to power AI-native search and reports meaningful reductions in support ticket volume, while manufacturing clients using Gaamma have gained real-time operational visibility that manual, spreadsheet-driven processes couldn't provide. These aren't hypothetical use cases — they reflect the same infrastructure-first approach applied to different industries with different operational needs.

## Future Directions

As agentic AI matures, Zunkiree Labs is continuing to push further into autonomous, multi-step automation — agents that don't just answer questions but complete entire workflows with minimal human intervention, expanding what GaaS (Agentic-as-a-Service) can practically deliver for businesses. Continued investment in data infrastructure and language capability remains central to that roadmap, since every new capability depends on the same foundation: clean, accessible, AI-ready data.

## Conclusion

From AI-native search to agentic commerce and enterprise automation, Zunkiree Labs' innovations share a common thread — infrastructure built to compound in value over time, not one-off integrations. For a closer look at what's next, exploring the company's products and ongoing work is the best way to stay current as this space keeps moving.
