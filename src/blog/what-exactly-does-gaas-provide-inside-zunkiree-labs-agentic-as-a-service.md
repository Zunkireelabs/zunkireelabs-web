## The Short Answer

**GaaS — Agentic-as-a-Service — provides AI agents that do the work, not software you operate yourself.** Instead of logging into another tool and clicking through tasks, you get autonomous agents that connect your CRM, email and marketing systems and complete work across all of them. At [Zunkiree Labs](https://zunkireelabs.com/resources/what-is-gaas/), that means AI agents, RAG search and voice agents built for businesses worldwide, delivered as outcomes rather than a dashboard you have to drive. The rest of this article unpacks exactly what sits inside that promise.

## From Software You Operate to Work That Gets Done

The clearest way to understand what GaaS provides is to contrast it with the model most teams know. Traditional software — the SaaS model — hands you a tool and expects you to supply the labour: you open the application, read the screen, decide what to do and perform each step by hand. The software is capable, but nothing happens until a person makes it happen.

GaaS flips that arrangement. The platform supplies the labour through AI agents that act across systems instead of one at a time. Zunkiree Labs' orchestration layer, **Orca**, connects your CRM, email and marketing tools into one coordinated layer with customised automation workflows tailored to how your business actually operates. Rather than a human moving information between a CRM, an inbox and a scheduling tool, the agents coordinate those systems directly and carry a task through to completion. If you want the fuller distinction between the two models, Zunkiree Labs breaks it down in [its guide on the key differences between GaaS and SaaS](https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/understanding-the-key-differences-between-gaas-and-saas/).

## What's Actually Inside a GaaS Deployment

"Agentic-as-a-Service" can sound abstract, so it helps to name the concrete components a real deployment provides. With Zunkiree Labs, a GaaS engagement is built on a few distinct layers that work together:

1. **Pre-built AI applications** — applications already proven in production, not concepts and not pilots. These are starting points that have run real workloads, which shortens the distance between signing on and seeing results.
2. **Production deployment** — tested, monitored systems shipped on schedule using reusable accelerators. The emphasis is on getting agents into live operation with proper monitoring, rather than leaving them as experiments.
3. **Orchestration via [Orca](https://zunkireelabs.com/products/orca/)** — the layer that ties your CRM, email and marketing tools together so agents can act across systems in coordinated workflows.
4. **Scale to any use case** — infrastructure that grows with demand and extends to new use cases without rebuilding from scratch.

Together these pieces are what "the service" in Agentic-as-a-Service really refers to: not a single feature, but a production-grade stack that takes an agent from idea to monitored, shipped, scalable operation. For worked illustrations, Zunkiree Labs' overview of [what Agentic-as-a-Service looks like in practice](https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/gaas-examples-what-agentic-as-a-service-looks-like-in-practice/) shows these layers at work.

## GaaS in Production Across Industries

A platform is only as convincing as the work it actually performs, and this is where GaaS stops being a definition and becomes operational. Zunkiree Labs runs four verticals on one shared platform, each already live in production.

In **education**, the platform runs for consultancies and study-abroad recruiters, with Admizz's education CRM deployment reporting 45% faster response times running live today. In **healthcare**, it is built for Australian providers — hospitals, aged care and community care — replacing manual shift rostering, credential tracking and workforce-to-recruitment handoff with one connected system. In **real estate**, it is built for CRE sponsor firms raising capital from investors, providing secure investor data rooms, offerings and commitments tracking designed for capital-raise workflows rather than a generic CRM. And for **agencies**, Zunkiree Labs runs its own deal pipeline, proposals, delivery and time tracking on this same platform, with Mobilise running the same infrastructure externally. That last point is the most telling: the company operates its own business on the exact system it sells, which is a stronger signal of what GaaS provides than any brochure claim.

## How Zunkiree Labs Delivers GaaS

What distinguishes a serious GaaS provider from a vendor simply re-labelling automation is whether the agents reach production and stay monitored there. Zunkiree Labs is an AI development company based in Kathmandu building for businesses worldwide, and its approach centres on shipping tested, monitored systems on schedule with reusable accelerators — the opposite of perpetual pilots that never leave the lab.

The practical value for a buyer is that you are not handed a blank platform and left to figure it out. You get pre-built applications proven in production, Orca's orchestration connecting your existing tools, and infrastructure engineered to scale to any use case as your needs grow. Because the four verticals share one platform, a healthcare rostering workflow and an education enrolment workflow draw on the same proven foundation rather than one-off builds. If your next step is deciding whether an Agentic-as-a-Service model fits your operations, the honest answer is that GaaS provides finished work, running live, across your connected systems — and that is precisely what Zunkiree Labs sets out to deliver.
