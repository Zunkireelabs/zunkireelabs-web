**In short:** A CRM for a study abroad consultancy is a system that follows each student from first enquiry to confirmed enrollment: capturing inquiries, collecting documents, tracking applications to each partner college, managing offers, following visa progress and confirming enrollment. A spreadsheet or inbox can do this for a few dozen students. Past that, deadlines slip, status gets lost between counsellors and students fall through the gaps. This guide sets out what to track, how to structure stages and what to look for in a system.

## Key Takeaways

- Model the **whole journey,** not just leads: inquiry, counselling, documents, application, offer, acceptance, visa, enrollment.
- Give every student **one record** and every stage **one owner,** so nothing is waiting on "someone".
- **Applications are many-to-one.** One student may apply to several colleges, each with its own status.
- **Deadlines drive the work.** Track intake dates, document due dates and offer expiry as first-class fields with reminders.
- **Response speed matters** in the early stage, because an unanswered inquiry cools quickly.
- Choose a system you can **configure to your process,** not one that forces a generic sales pipeline.

## The Pipeline, Stage by Stage

| Stage | What happens | What to track |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry | Student contacts you by form, phone, chat or social | Source, preferred country and course, time received, first response time |
| Counselling | Needs, budget and eligibility discussed | Counsellor, notes, shortlist of courses, follow-up date |
| Document collection | Transcripts, tests, identity and statements gathered | Checklist per document, received, verified, missing, expiry |
| Application | Submitted to one or more colleges | College, course, intake, submission date, reference number, status |
| Offer | College issues conditional or unconditional offer | Offer type, conditions, expiry date, acceptance status |
| Acceptance and deposit | Student accepts and meets conditions | Conditions met, payment status, confirmation |
| Visa | Visa application prepared and followed | Stage, submitted date, requests for information, outcome |
| Enrollment | Student confirmed at the institution | Confirmation, start date, handover to post-arrival support |

Adapt the stages to your market. The point is that each has a clear entry and exit, a named owner and a due date.

## Data Model Essentials

- **Student** (contact details, preferences, source, counsellor).
- **Documents** with type, status and expiry.
- **Applications** linked to the student, each with its own college, course, intake and status.
- **Offers** linked to applications.
- **Visa cases** linked to the student and destination.
- **Partner colleges** with contacts and requirements.
- **Activities and notes** so any colleague can pick up the thread.

Avoid storing status in free text. Use controlled values so you can report accurately.

## What Goes Wrong Without a System

- Students ask the same question to three people and get three answers.
- A document is received by email and never logged.
- An offer expires because nobody saw the date.
- You cannot say how many applications are stuck waiting on documents.
- Managers cannot tell which marketing source produces enrolments.
- A counsellor leaves and their students' history leaves with them.

## Where AI Helps

AI is useful on the repetitive edges of the process, with people kept in charge of advice:

- **Answering routine questions** about requirements, deadlines and documents from approved information, instantly and at any hour. Our guide to [AI for education consultancies](/blog/ai-for-education-consultancy-student-inquiry-automation-guide/) covers this.
- **Triage and routing** of new inquiries to the right counsellor.
- **Document checklists** that flag what is missing.
- **Summaries** of long email threads for the next person.
- **Reminders** for deadlines and offers about to expire.

Keep advice on admissions and visas with qualified staff, show the date that information was last checked, and send complex cases to a person with the context attached.

## What to Look for in a System

1. **Configurable stages and fields** to match your process.
2. **Multiple applications per student,** with status per college.
3. **Document tracking** with checklists and expiry.
4. **Deadline reminders** tied to owners.
5. **Visa stage tracking** alongside the student record.
6. **Partner-college records** and communications in one place.
7. **Campaign and source tracking** connected to real enrolments.
8. **Role-based access** and an audit trail for student data.
9. **Reporting** by stage, counsellor, source and country.
10. **Integration** with email, forms and messaging you already use.

## Setup Checklist

1. Write down your actual stages and who owns each.
2. List the documents required per destination.
3. Decide the controlled statuses for applications, offers and visas.
4. Migrate current students, starting with active cases.
5. Set reminders for deadlines and offer expiry.
6. Train counsellors, and review the reports weekly.
