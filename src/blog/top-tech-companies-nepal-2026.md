## How We Selected These Companies

This list reflects companies meeting specific criteria:

### Inclusion Criteria

1. **Operating in Nepal**: Significant engineering or product team based in Nepal
2. **Technology Focus**: Core business involves building or applying technology
3. **Scale or Impact**: Either meaningful team size (20+) or demonstrated market impact
4. **Active in 2026**: Currently operating and hiring

### Research Methodology

- Company websites and LinkedIn profiles
- Job posting analysis
- Industry interviews
- Public financial data where available
- Media coverage and press releases

### What This List Doesn't Include

- Pure consulting firms without product/engineering teams
- Companies with <20 employees unless notable
- Multinational branch offices with limited Nepal presence
- Companies primarily focused on hardware sales
