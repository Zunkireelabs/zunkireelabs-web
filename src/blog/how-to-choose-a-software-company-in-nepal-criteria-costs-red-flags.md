**Short answer:** to choose a software company in Nepal, shortlist from a directory, then judge each firm on checkable evidence: registered legal status, named client work you can call, the team that will actually build your project, a clear engagement model and written terms on code and data ownership. Rankings and "best company" lists are a starting point, not a verdict.

## Start With a Shortlist, Not a Ranking

Directories help you find names. Our own [Top 50 IT and Software Companies in Nepal](/blog/top-tech-companies-nepal-2026/) is grouped by category and explains how each company was selected. Use it to build a list of three to five firms, then compare them on the points below. For a buyer's overview, see our guide to [IT companies in Nepal](/it-companies-nepal/).

## Criteria That Matter

- **Fit for your work.** A firm that builds mobile apps is not automatically right for AI agents or a multi-tenant SaaS. Ask for work similar to yours.
- **Named, checkable clients.** Ask for a reference call. Claims without a named client are marketing.
- **The real team.** Ask who will build your project, their experience and how handover works.
- **Legal and certification checks.** Nepal's company registry is the [Office of the Company Registrar](https://ocr.gov.np); ask for the registration certificate and check the details on its portal, since the available search tools change. Verify any partner status with the issuer too. Our [vendor checklist](/blog/how-to-check-a-nepali-it-company-before-you-hire-it/) walks through this.
- **Communication and time zones.** Agree meeting overlap, reporting and a single point of contact.

## Engagement Models

Nepali software companies commonly offer three arrangements: a dedicated team working as an extension of yours, a fixed-price project with a defined scope, and white-label delivery for agencies. Each suits a different kind of project, and we compare them in [dedicated team vs fixed price vs white label](/blog/software-outsourcing-models-dedicated-team-vs-fixed-price-vs-white-label/). Zunkiree Labs offers these through its [software outsourcing](/solutions/software-outsourcing/) service.

## How Pricing Works

Prices vary widely with scope, team seniority and model, so quotes from different firms are only comparable if they describe the same scope. Ask each firm for a written breakdown (discovery, build, testing, handover, support) rather than one number, and ask what is excluded. We do not publish a price range here because we cannot support one with a verified source.

## Red Flags

- No named clients or references
- A fixed price offered with no written scope
- Reluctance to state who owns the code and data
- Vague answers on who will do the work
- Pressure to sign before a paid discovery or pilot
