## Introduction to AI in Nepal

AI has reshaped how businesses operate globally, and Nepal's startup scene is no longer sitting on the sidelines of that shift. A small but growing group of Kathmandu-based companies is building genuine AI infrastructure — not just applying AI tools built elsewhere — with the potential to change how local businesses handle search, customer support, commerce, and operations.

## Current Landscape of AI Startups

Nepal's AI startups are concentrated around a few core areas: AI-native search and retrieval, agentic automation for commerce and operations, and industry-specific applications like healthcare and manufacturing. Zunkiree Labs is one example, building AI-native search, an AI commerce agent (Stella), and an agentic ERP platform (Gaamma) from Kathmandu for both local and international clients — proof that infrastructure-level AI products don't require being based in Silicon Valley.

## Key Players in the Nepali AI Startup Scene

Founded in 2018, Zunkiree Labs has grown from a small Kathmandu team into a company serving enterprise clients internationally, built around the belief that product-first, AI-native companies could emerge from Nepal rather than only providing outsourced services to foreign firms. Alongside Zunkiree Labs, [a growing list of AI companies in Nepal](https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/top-ai-companies-nepal-2026/) is building in adjacent spaces — evidence that this isn't a single company's story but an emerging ecosystem.

## Challenges Facing AI Startups

Nepali AI startups face familiar early-stage hurdles, sharpened by local conditions: funding is harder to access than in more mature startup markets, competing for AI engineering talent against better-resourced international employers is a real constraint, and regulatory frameworks for AI are still catching up to the pace of the technology. Startups that survive these pressures tend to be the ones focused on a clear, defensible product rather than chasing every possible AI application at once.

## Opportunities for Growth

Despite the challenges, the opportunities are real: international clients increasingly value AI vendors who can deliver quality engineering at competitive cost, and Nepal's growing pool of technical talent — combined with lower operating costs than markets like the US or Europe — creates room for AI startups to compete globally rather than staying purely domestic. Partnerships with international clients, as Zunkiree Labs has built, show that geography is becoming less of a barrier for AI-native companies than it used to be.

## Future Trends in AI in Nepal

As detailed in our broader look at [the state of AI in Nepal for 2026](https://zunkireelabs.com/blog/state-of-ai-nepal-2026/), continued growth in AI adoption locally and abroad is likely to keep expanding the market for Nepal-based AI startups — particularly those building agentic, infrastructure-level products rather than thin AI feature wrappers. The startups positioned to benefit most are the ones investing in real technical depth now, ahead of the demand curve.

## Conclusion

Nepal's AI startup ecosystem is still young, but it's no longer speculative — real companies are shipping real AI products to real clients, both locally and internationally. Nurturing that ecosystem, through funding, talent development, and supportive policy, is what will determine whether Nepal becomes a genuine contributor to the global AI landscape rather than just a consumer of AI built elsewhere.
