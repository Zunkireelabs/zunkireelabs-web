## How this list was made
<p>Every event below was checked against the organizers' own page or an official listing, and the dates, venues and organizers are quoted as those sources give them. Where sources disagreed or only an event aggregator listed something, we left it out. This list was checked on 2 October 2026; schedules change, so confirm with the organizers before you plan travel.</p>

## Still ahead: PgConf.Nepal 2026
<p>PgConf.Nepal 2026 runs from 18 to 21 November 2026 at the Pulchowk Campus of Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu, in English. It is described as Nepal's fourth international PostgreSQL conference, with two days of workshops and two days of talks. It is co-organized by the Nepal PostgreSQL user group (NP-PUG), Kathmandu University, Tribhuvan University and Postgres Professional, and its call for papers was listed as open at pgconf.org.np. Source: <a href="https://www.postgresql.org/about/event/pgconfnepal-2026-2590/" rel="noopener">the PostgreSQL project's event listing</a>.</p>

## Already held in 2026
<p><strong>AI Conf 2026</strong> took place on 10 and 11 January 2026 in Kathmandu. It was organized by the team behind Web Weekend Kathmandu (WWKTM) and focused on the practical design, deployment and governance of AI systems. Source: <a href="https://english.onlinekhabar.com/kathmandu-to-host-ai-conf-2026-with-global-ai-experts-and-builders.html" rel="noopener">Onlinekhabar</a>.</p>
<p><strong>AI Summit Nepal 2026</strong> was a one-day summit on 5 April 2026 in Lalitpur. It was hosted by The Startup Network Nepal, in collaboration with Oorja World, with the US Embassy in Nepal as a strategic partner, and looked at the role of AI in Nepal's economic and technological development. Source: <a href="https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/28135/the-startup-network-nepal-to-host-ai-summit-nepal-2026-on-april-5" rel="noopener">B360 Nepal</a>.</p>
<p><strong>WordCamp Kathmandu 2026</strong> was held on 18 and 19 September 2026 at Alice Receptions, Gairidhara, Kathmandu, with a Contributor Day on 18 September and the conference on 19 September. Its theme was "Where Ancient Culture Meets Open Source". Source: <a href="https://kathmandu.wordcamp.org/2026/" rel="noopener">the WordCamp Kathmandu 2026 site</a>.</p>

## What this list does not cover
<p>It is not a complete calendar. Smaller meetups and company events are not included, and events announced after 2 October 2026 will not appear here until this page is updated. An edition for 2027 should be written when the organizers publish their dates, not before.</p>
