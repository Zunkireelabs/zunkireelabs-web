**In short:** Speaking at The New York Times DealBook Summit, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella predicted that AI agents will reshuffle companies whose business model was built for the "human web", especially those that earn money from advertising and eyeballs. TechCrunch reported his remarks on 18 November 2026. The talk was a prediction, not data, and the article gives no statistics. For a business, the practical question is whether AI agents can find, read and act on your website.

## Key Takeaways

- Nadella argued that the shift to an agentic web could change which companies pay others for traffic and for transactions, comparing it to the move from portals such as Yahoo to search engines such as Google.
- He said companies monetising ads through eyeballs may have to move toward cost-per-click or more transactional models.
- His warning to businesses: "if you don't accept the agent, somebody else will."
- TechCrunch reports that Amazon has decided to block AI agents from its retail website. The article attributes this only to the DealBook discussion and cites no Amazon statement.
- The article contains no numbers. Treat it as an executive's forecast.

## What Did Nadella Say?

According to [TechCrunch's report](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/satya-nadella-predicts-ai-agents-will-reshuffle-the-economics-of-the-web) by Sarah Perez, Nadella said "the current aggregators who have a business model which was for the human web will get, in some sense, reshuffled." On monetisation he said that if you were earning from ads because of eyeballs, you may now have to get down to "maybe CPC wins; maybe it's more transactional."

He also compared the change to earlier shifts, such as portals giving way to search engines, when what companies pay for, and who they pay, changed.

## What About Amazon Blocking AI Agents?

TechCrunch states that Amazon decided to block AI agents from its retail site, which prevents them from browsing the catalogue and buying on a user's behalf. The article presents this as part of the DealBook discussion and does not cite an Amazon statement, a date or the scope of the block. Treat it as reported, not confirmed by Amazon.

Nadella's argument, as the article paraphrases it, is that a company protecting its existing model risks losing to rivals that welcome agents. The article's example is hypothetical: if Amazon keeps blocking agents, a platform such as Shopify could allow them and gain an advantage. It does not say Shopify does so.

## Why Does This Matter for a Business Website?

Nadella's comments were about large platforms, but the direction applies to any site that gets customers from search. If a buyer's AI assistant researches suppliers, compares options or books a service, then the assistant reads your site instead of the buyer. Three practical points follow:

- **Be readable to machines.** Clear page structure, accurate structured data and plain-language answers help both search engines and AI assistants. See [what generative engine optimization is](/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo-vs-aeo-vs-seo/).
- **Decide which crawlers and agents you allow.** Blocking is a business decision with trade-offs. Our guide to [AI crawlers and robots.txt](/blog/ai-crawlers-and-robots-txt-gptbot-oai-searchbot-claudebot-perplexitybot/) lists what each agent name does.
- **Measure what AI systems say about you.** Use a simple monthly check, described in [how to measure AI search visibility](/blog/how-to-measure-ai-search-visibility-a-simple-monthly-method/).

If you want to let customers transact through agents, that means exposing bookings, quotes or catalogues in a form software can use, with limits on what an agent may do. That is the kind of bounded-authority design we build in [custom AI agents](/solutions/custom-agent/).

## What Is Still Unknown?

The article gives no adoption figures, no timeline and no evidence that agent-driven traffic is replacing human traffic today. It also does not describe how ad pricing would change in practice. Nadella's remarks describe where he expects the market to go. Check your own analytics for AI referrals before changing strategy.
