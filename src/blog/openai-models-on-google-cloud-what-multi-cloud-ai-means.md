**In short:** On 21 October 2026 OpenAI announced that its frontier models and Codex agentic workflows are coming to Google Cloud's Model Garden. Google Cloud says the models are rolling out in private preview to select enterprise customers, and OpenAI says general availability is planned "in the coming weeks" without a date. Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner. For buyers, the news matters less as a model release than as a sign that where you run a model is becoming a choice rather than a lock-in.

## Key Takeaways

- OpenAI and Google Cloud are bringing OpenAI's frontier models and Codex workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden.
- Availability is private preview for select enterprise customers, with an interest form for others. General availability has no stated date.
- Customers with existing Google Cloud spending commitments can apply them to eligible OpenAI purchases, which are sold through Google Cloud Marketplace.
- OpenAI says Microsoft remains its primary cloud partner. Coverage differs on what that means for exclusivity.
- Secondary coverage names specific models, but the names are not confirmed in the excerpt of OpenAI's own post that we could read, so check the Model Garden card before relying on them.

## What Was Announced

OpenAI's post, titled "OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud", says the two companies are partnering to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex agentic workflows to Google Cloud's Model Garden. Google Cloud's statement, quoted in coverage, says the models are "rolling out now in private preview to select enterprise customers" and offers an interest form to everyone else. OpenAI says general availability is "planned in the coming weeks".

On purchasing, customers who already have commitments with Google Cloud can apply them toward eligible OpenAI models, and sales go through Google Cloud Marketplace.

## What Is Confirmed and What Is Not

- **Confirmed by the companies:** the partnership, the Model Garden destination, private preview status, "coming weeks" for general availability, the Marketplace route and Microsoft's continuing role as primary partner.
- **Reported but check before relying on it:** the specific model names. Secondary sources list GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, and one guide describes access through Vertex AI while most sources say Model Garden.
- **Not stated:** a general availability date, regions, prices on Google Cloud, and any change to OpenAI's Microsoft agreement. One analysis says the arrangement ends Azure's exclusive distribution of OpenAI's frontier models. Another notes OpenAI models have already been available on Amazon Web Services since April, which would put them on all three of the largest US clouds.

## Why Buyers Should Care

1. **Less lock-in to one cloud.** If a model is available on more than one cloud, your cloud choice can follow your existing contracts, data location and security setup instead of the model.
2. **Existing commitments can stretch further.** Being able to spend a Google Cloud commitment on OpenAI models changes procurement for companies already committed there.
3. **Model choice sits next to your data.** Running models in the same cloud as your data can simplify access controls and reduce data movement, which matters for privacy rules.
4. **Wait for terms.** Private preview is not general availability, and no Google Cloud pricing was published.

## What to Do Now

- **Do not re-platform on an announcement.** Keep your application logic and tool definitions independent of any one provider, so a model or cloud can change without a rebuild.
- **Register interest only if it helps you.** If you are a Google Cloud customer evaluating OpenAI models, the interest form is free.
- **Compare on your own task.** Run the same representative workload on each option and measure cost and accuracy yourself.
- **Check data terms.** Confirm where prompts and outputs are processed and retained before sending real customer data.

## The Short Version

OpenAI on Google Cloud is a distribution change more than a model change. It gives Google Cloud customers a way to buy OpenAI models inside their existing agreement, while Microsoft stays primary. Treat it as preview news, wait for dates and prices, and keep your own systems portable.
